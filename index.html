More about what we do

NouriLoop: AI built in from the ground up

NouriLoop is a nutrition-tracking web app designed around AI from its first line of code, not one with a chatbot added later. AI powers eight of its features:

  • reading a meal, a recipe or a supplement label from a photo
  • turning a goal described in plain words into a measurable one, then checking in on it
  • explaining glucose responses from a continuous glucose monitor
  • "what if" predictions from your own data
  • a help assistant that answers from the app's own pages

Underneath, it's built so cost stays a choice:

  • Pick the model that fits the budget. Use Anthropic, any of the many models available through OpenRouter, or a model on your own Ollama server with no per-call fees.
  • Simple tasks use smaller models. The help assistant runs on a smaller, cheaper model automatically. Only the work that needs a large model gets one.
  • Test before trusting a cheaper model. A vision benchmark checks whether a model reads food photos well enough before it's used.
  • Every AI call is metered, so the cost of each feature is known, not guessed.
  • People review every estimate before it's saved.

We design client systems the same way. No organization should be locked into one AI vendor's prices.

It was built mostly with AI coding agents, with every step planned, directed and reviewed by a person. That's the process our developer course teaches. Open source under AGPL, and live at app.nouriloop.com.
