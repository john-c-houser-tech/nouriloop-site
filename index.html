What we do

Help your staff use AI tools well

Most organizations don't have an adoption problem; they have an expectations problem. Nobody is sure what's allowed, what "done" means, or who's accountable. We fix that with a short session for mixed audiences and a one-page standard — tuned to your needs — your team can adopt the same week.

Give your developers gates, not guesswork

AI coding agents produce more code than any person can review. The answer is a written process — plan approval, tests shown, a second review, a human at the merge — and skills that travel between projects. We bring a starter kit and leave your team running it.

Retire the technical debt you've been living with

Old applications, undocumented scripts, the system only one person understands. We rebuild and document them with AI-assisted development, under your review, on your repositories, at a pace that was not possible two years ago.

Training

Working with AI Tools: What to Expect, What to Require 50 minutes, any audience. What these tools are and aren't, why they fail, how to make failures rare and harmless, and the three rules that make AI use safe in a public organization. Leaves you with a one-page AI Use Standard and a Quality Procedures checklist.

Coding with Agents Without Losing Control One day or two half-days, for developers and the people who review their work. Context, repository instructions, skills, process rules, and review gates. Leaves you with a starter skill library and an instructions-file template, installed in your own repository before you go home.

Both sessions align with your existing policies rather than replacing them. If anyone on your team needs materials in a different format, captioning, or other support to take part, ask — it's provided at no cost.

About

John Houser has spent 37 years in library and public-sector systems: systems administration, integration, and development. Open-source software he wrote is still in use at Oregon library systems. Most recently he has been a senior systems administrator in the Multnomah County IT Division. In the last year he has used AI coding agents to ship a production web application, two games, and more than ten internal tools, and has written the process rules that made that possible without losing control. NouriLoop LLC is based in Astoria, Oregon.

The name: you can nourish an organization the way you nourish a person — steadily, with the right inputs and a strong feedback loop.
