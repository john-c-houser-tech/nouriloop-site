About

John Houser has spent 37 years in library and public-sector systems: systems administration, integration and development. Open-source software he wrote is still in production at Oregon library systems. Most recently he has been a senior systems administrator in the Multnomah County IT Division. In the last year he has rebuilt or extended more than ten internal applications there, most of them his own hand-written code, with AI help. He has also built a production web application with AI at its core and two games, and has written the process rules that keep AI-assisted work under a person's control. NouriLoop LLC is based in Astoria, Oregon.

How we work

  • Plain talk. No hype about what AI can do, and no doom either. If a tool isn't right for the job, we'll say so.
  • Your systems, your repositories, your review. Everything we build is documented and handed over. Nothing depends on us to keep running.
  • Local when it helps. Based in Astoria, working across Clatsop County and the Pacific Northwest, in person when that's better.
